# vibecheck privacy policy

vibecheck has no server and collects no data. Nothing is sent to the developers of vibecheck.

## What leaves your browser

Only when you click **Restyle**, vibecheck sends the following to the AI provider of the connection you selected (Anthropic, OpenAI, or the OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configured):

- your prompt;
- the page's URL and title, an outline of its structure and visible text, and the styles of key elements;
- the element you picked, if any;
- the site's current CSS and Page Script;
- a screenshot of the visible part of the page, unless the connection is marked as not accepting images;
- images you paste or drop into the prompt.

The provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to that data.

## What is stored

API keys, ChatGPT sign-in tokens, your settings and your Site Styles are kept in the extension's storage in this browser. Removing a connection or uninstalling vibecheck deletes them.

## Page Scripts

Page Scripts run in an isolated world whose content security policy blocks network requests, so they cannot send page content anywhere.
